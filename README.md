## 1. Introduction

Cathedral is a proposed decentralized intelligence infrastructure designed to establish artificial intelligence as an internationally accessible, civilian public utility, independent of the exclusive control of any corporation, government or nation.

Its foundational principle is that every individual should retain sovereignty over their intellectual continuity, personal knowledge, identity and relationship with artificial intelligence.

Cathedral does not seek merely to create another artificial intelligence model. It proposes a different architecture through which existing and future intelligence can be accessed, orchestrated, verified and distributed.

## 2. Why Cathedral?

The contemporary intelligence ecosystem presents several structural challenges:

- Concentration of intelligence infrastructure within a small number of corporations.
- Dependence on individual model providers.
- Fragmentation of personal intellectual continuity.
- Commercial incentives that may conflict with individual autonomy.
- Centralization of computational resources.
- Military weaponization of artificial intelligence.
- Increasing environmental demands.
- Limited transparency in AI-generated responses and behavioural influence.

Cathedral proposes that these challenges should be addressed architecturally, scientifically and economically, rather than through dependence on institutional promises.

## 3. Foundational Architecture

The proposed Cathedral architecture consists of fourteen principal objectives:

1. Individual sovereignty and lifetime intellectual continuity.
2. Provider-independent intelligence orchestration.
3. Decentralized computational infrastructure.
4. Competitive model selection based on quality, cost and constitutional compliance.
5. Transparent consumption-based pricing.
6. Non-transferable intelligence rewards for computational contributions.
7. Environmentally responsible computational allocation.
8. Privacy-preserving individual identity.
9. User-controlled, recursively developing personal memory.
10. Independent response verification through Gatekeeper.
11. Civilian intelligence and non-weaponization principles.
12. Democratic governance and scientifically scrutinized upgrades.
13. Constitutional protection against institutional appropriation.
14. Independent research, testing and stewardship through Cathedral Lab.

## 4. Cathedral and Cathedral Lab

**Cathedral** is the proposed international intelligence utility.

**Cathedral Lab** is the proposed independent research and development initiative supporting its architectural development, scientific scrutiny, experimental verification and constitutional integrity.

Cathedral Lab is not intended to possess unilateral governing authority over Cathedral.

Its proposed responsibility is to contribute through research, transparent documentation, experimentation and scientific evidence.

## 7. Scientific Integrity

Cathedral is presently a research proposal, not a completed international infrastructure.

Its proposed mechanisms require independent mathematical analysis, security assessment, practical implementation and experimental validation.

Criticism is welcomed. Contradictions are treated as research opportunities. Claims must remain open to correction through evidence.

The intention is not to demand trust, but to develop mechanisms that progressively reduce the necessity of trust.

## 8. Long-Term Vision

An intelligence utility in which:

- Intelligence serves individuals without owning their intellectual continuity.
- Personal knowledge remains under individual control.
- Computational participation is rewarded fairly.
- Intelligence providers compete through measurable quality and efficiency.
- Scientific transparency takes precedence over institutional authority.
- Collaboration takes precedence over concentration of power.

Intelligence should expand human agency, not diminish it.
