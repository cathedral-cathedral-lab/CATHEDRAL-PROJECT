## Introduction

Cathedral proposes a decentralized intelligence utility designed to serve humanity through individual sovereignty, lifelong intellectual continuity, competitive computation, privacy-preserving identity and civilian AI governance.

Its fundamental objective is to establish an architecture in which access to intelligence does not require surrendering ownership of one's intellectual history to any individual corporation, institution or government.

## Architectural Foundations

1. Individual sovereignty and lifelong continuity.
2. Intelligence-provider independence.
3. Decentralized computational infrastructure.
4. Competitive, consumption-based pricing.
5. Privacy-preserving sovereign identity.
6. Non-transferable intelligence rewards.
7. Environmental responsibility.
8. Independent response verification through Gatekeeper.
9. Civilian intelligence and non-weaponization.
10. Transparent constitutional governance.

## Invitation to Collaborate

Cathedral invites scientists, mathematicians, cryptographers, economists, researchers, software architects and independent builders to examine, challenge and develop its proposed architecture.

Constructive criticism, mathematical formalization, scientific research and experimental validation are welcomed.

This proposal is not presented as a completed or independently validated international system. Its mechanisms remain subject to scientific scrutiny and practical testing.

## Cathedral Lab

Cathedral Lab is the proposed independent research steward of Cathedral. Its intended purpose is to research, test and evaluate architectural developments without exercising unilateral authority over the proposed public utility.
